## Coverage limits

- This run was bounded and is not exhaustive. Unsuccessful searches and unreachable pages are not evidence that nothing exists.
- The register still holds only about 10 U.S. state and local records, so many enacted state laws are likely missing. This run could not close that gap. Texas, Utah, Virginia, Washington, Maryland, Minnesota and Tennessee were not searched, and no attorney-general guidance was searched. New York City and other local rules were not re-checked. State consultations and rulemakings were not covered. Official portals (nysenate.gov, ilga.gov, cga.ct.gov) were blocked, so legislature text could not be read.
- Americas: Argentina, Peru, Uruguay, Costa Rica, Panama, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Paraguay, the Caribbean and Central America were not searched beyond the register audit. White House executive orders and Congress.gov AI bills, Canadian provincial and regulator items (OPC, OSFI), Canada Bill C-8, and Brazil and Chile regulator guidance were not searched.
- Asia-Pacific: New Zealand, Taiwan, Philippines, Malaysia, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Pacific island states and Hong Kong were not covered. India and Australia had nothing confirmed in the window. AI-relevant cybersecurity regulation and the Singapore CSA agentic AI addendum were not checked.
- Middle East and Africa: Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait, Israel, Jordan, Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia, Ghana, Rwanda, Ethiopia, Senegal and Tanzania were not covered, and official gazette checks for the UAE and Nigeria were not done. Regional AI-relevant cybersecurity regulation was not covered.
- Europe: the United Kingdom (no October-November item verified), the Council of Europe Framework Convention, Ukraine, Serbia, the Netherlands, Ireland, Spain, France and other member-state implementing laws were not covered. EU cybersecurity instruments (NIS2, CRA, Cybersecurity Act revision) were not covered. Most official sites could not be reached directly; nothing dated on or after 2026-10-01 was verified apart from page-update dates.
- Verification used fetched page text only. Where a page was truncated (for example EUR-Lex Article 113) the affected claims were held back.
