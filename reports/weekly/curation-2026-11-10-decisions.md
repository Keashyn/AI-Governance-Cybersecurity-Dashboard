## Id mapping (rule: US-<bill or public-law identifier>-<year>)

Bills use the bill number and the year of introduction, because bill numbers restart every Congress. Public laws use the public-law number (PL119-n) and the year of enactment: the PL number is the enacted instrument's own permanent citation and matches the govinfo sourceUrl, while the bill number names the bill and is kept in the title. Writing it as `PL119-12` keeps the first segment from looking like a two-letter state code.
