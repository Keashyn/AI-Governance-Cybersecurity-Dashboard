How to read the register

Formally adopted means the instrument has been officially approved; In force means effective at least in part. Proposed covers draft legislation or rules, while Consultation is an open or recently closed request for comment. Adopted policy identifies approved government strategies and policies that are not statutes. Guidance is official explanatory material and is not represented as binding legislation. Lapsed and Withdrawn identify inactive proposals. Superseded, Repealed and Expired identify instruments no longer marked current by the source catalog.

Standards and frameworks are reference material, not laws. Their appearance with a country filter does not mean that a country adopted them or that they are legally required; legal force depends on local incorporation or other applicable rules.

Titles are shown in English where an English rendering is available. Where the source title is known, it appears below the English title for reference; translations are for navigation and do not replace the linked official text. Dates and summaries are sourced from the issuing authority or catalog. “Last checked” refers to the source poll, not a guarantee that every page or language version was reviewed. This tracker is informational, not legal advice.
