How to read the register

Enacted means formally adopted; In force means effective at least in part. Proposed covers draft legislation or rules, while Consultation is an open or recently closed request for comment. Guidance is official explanatory material and is not represented as binding legislation.

Dates and summaries are sourced from the issuing authority. “Last checked” refers to the source poll, not a guarantee that every page or language version was reviewed. This tracker is informational, not legal advice.
